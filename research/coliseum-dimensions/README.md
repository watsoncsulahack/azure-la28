# Los Angeles Memorial Coliseum: dimensions and drawing research

Reviewed November 27, 2026. Scope: the **Los Angeles Memorial Coliseum**, USC's stadium in Exposition Park, rather than the Colosseum in Rome. Research for a reference-based 3D reconstruction; no model was built during this investigation.

**We found useful floor layouts, a dimensioned field drawing, a dimensioned stadium section, and a credible location for original architectural records. We did not locate a complete, publicly downloadable, current as-built blueprint set or a survey establishing every wall and elevation.**

Open [the searchable dimension explorer](dimension-explorer.html), [measurement catalog](dimensions.csv), [source register](sources.csv), or [drawing index](drawing-index.csv). Each measurement has its source, PDF page, configuration status, interpretation limits and metric conversion. The original feet-and-inches values take precedence over rounded metric displays.

## The strongest finds

### 1. The stadium's own Technical Guide — Draft 2024

[Official PDF, 65 pages](https://lacoliseum.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/Coliseum_Technical_Guide_Draft2024.pdf) — source **S01**.

This is the most useful post-renovation starting point located. It includes:

- A site plan showing the bowl, south-side tower, surrounding circulation and park context: **PDF page 5 / printed page 3**.
- Explicit overall heights, field/street relationships and area figures: **PDF page 8 / printed page 6**.
- A locker-room layout: **PDF page 9 / printed page 7**, followed by room areas on PDF page 10.
- Seven tower-level layouts: Level 1 on **PDF page 11**; Levels 2 and 3 together on **page 13**; Level 4 on **page 15**; Level 5 on **page 17**; Level 6 on **page 19**; rooftop Level 7 on **page 20**.
- Field dimensions and an overhead field photograph: **PDF page 21 / printed page 19**.
- An elevator service matrix: PDF page 31; gate/escalator descriptions: page 32; physical display sizes: page 43.

These are useful plans of real spaces, but they lack the complete dimension chains, structural details, spot elevations and survey control of construction/as-built drawings. The cover explicitly says **Draft 2024**. The site-plan page mentions a Google Drive download, but the PDF did not provide a usable link annotation for it. Its later heading “Floor Plans” concerns event-plan approval rather than an additional architectural drawing set.

### 2. An official guide with a field drawing dimensioned in feet and inches

[Promoter Guide, Rev 5.8 (12122020), 45 pages](https://lacoliseum.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/03/Promoter_Guide_5-6_r.pdf) — **S02**.

**PDF/printed page 37** is particularly valuable: a field-level plan with seating outlines, dimension lines, varying transverse widths and colored polygon areas. Its labels include:

| Annotated spans | Source labels |
|---|---|
| Seven transverse spans, ordered with dimension labels upright | 193 ft 5 in; 234 ft; 248 ft 2 in; 262 ft 3 in; 265 ft 7 in; 261 ft 4 in; 250 ft 7 in |
| Three longitudinal spans | 504 ft 6 in; 418 ft 5 in; 414 ft 1 in |
| Field-court spans | 160 ft 2 in × 80 ft 1 in |
| Other marked spans | 13 ft 6 in; 44 ft 8 in |
| Five labeled polygons | 63,984; 8,950; 9,232; 4,500; 7,724 square feet |

The drawing is embedded as an image; these annotations were read visually. **Its underlying drawing date is not established by the 2020 publication date.** These spans have different endpoints from a generic football rectangle. The cross-widths also differ from the 2024 guide's 250–222 ft range. Match the actual dimension-line endpoints and reconcile configurations before using them together. No georeferenced station positions were recovered.

Pages **39–45** add tower presentation plans, interior photographs and a small section locator. That locator illustrates which level is being shown; it is not a measured construction section. Event-space areas include 11,000 ft² on Founders Level 2, 7,628 ft² on Level 3, 9,390 ft² at Club Level, 11,714 ft² for the 1923 Club and 18,962 ft² for Heritage Concourse. These are not necessarily whole-floor gross areas.

### 3. A public dimensioned section through the seating bowl

[Coliseum Commission special meeting packet, December 8, 2016, 41 pages](https://assets-us-01.kc-usercontent.com/0234f496-d2b7-00b6-17a4-b43e949b70a2/23bd91e4-d48e-4280-a79b-0c2338f518db/Agenda%20Packet-SpecialMeeting%2012.08.16.pdf) — **S08**.

**PDF page 32**, labeled **B-4**, reproduces DLR Group / Historic Resources Group sheet **41**, dated **August 18, 2015**, and marked **Approved July 28, 2016**. It shows a section at a proposed video board, including the sloping bowl, supporting structure and reference lines labeled:

| Reference line | Labeled elevation |
|---|---:|
| Level One — Yard | 39 ft 6 in |
| Level Two — Founders Level 1 | 51 ft 6 in |
| Level Three — Founders Level 2 | 63 ft 6 in |
| Level Four — Club | 75 ft 6 in |
| Level Five — Suite | 101 ft 6 in |
| Level Six — Press | 115 ft 6 in |

**These are drawing elevations, with an unverified zero datum. They are not verified heights above the field or sea level.** Although the reference lines name tower levels, the section itself cuts through the bowl/video board, not the tower. This qualifies the preliminary description of “tower levels relative to the field”: the field relationship has not been established.

Page **30 / B-2** also describes the proposed bowl as having a **33-inch seating tread**. Both sheets are design records, not certification of the configuration built in 2019. A uniform bowl slope or current floor-to-floor heights should not be assumed from them.

### 4. Historic overall geometry from the National Park Service

[National Historic Landmark nomination, 1984, 24 pages](https://npgallery.nps.gov/NRHP/GetAsset/NHLS/84003866_text) — **S04**, **PDF page 2**, architectural description.

The description gives an approximate **1,038 × 738 ft** bowl ellipse and identifies the original construction systems. It also supplies older field dimensions and elevations. This is strong evidence for a historic baseline, but predates the 1993 field lowering and the 2019 south tower.

The same nomination provides a concrete archival lead: **PDF page 23** says Woodford and Bernard, successors to the Parkinson firm, allowed inspection of the original Coliseum and 1932-renovation drawings in 1984. It says some drawings were reproduced with the nomination, but **the downloaded 24-page text asset does not include those drawing reproductions**. The separate NPS photo endpoint returned a one-page notice saying its PDF had not been digitized. Do not mistake that notice for a recovered photo or drawing collection.

### 5. The likely archival route to original blueprints: The Huntington

[The Huntington's July 12, 2022 acquisition announcement](https://www.huntington.org/watch-read-listen/news/news-release-huntington-acquires-archive-leading-early-20th-century-la) — **S09**.

The Huntington acquired the John and Donald Parkinson archive from restoration architect Wm. Scott Field. It identifies the Memorial Coliseum among the projects documented, and describes nearly 20,000 original ink-on-linen drawings and blueprints within an archive spanning 1894–1994.

**This locates a highly relevant archive; it does not establish which Coliseum sheets survive, whether they are digitized, or whether the collection is currently processed and open.** The 2022 release said researcher access would follow processing. No current item-level Coliseum drawing inventory was verified in this search.

A useful research request would ask for the Parkinson archive's **1921–1923 Coliseum plans, 1930–1932 expansion plans, sections, elevations, seating-bowl geometry and drawing/sheet index**, plus current access and reproduction arrangements. Those records would describe historic configurations, requiring reconciliation with later alterations. No request or email has been sent.

For vertical modeling, the 2024 guide is internally consistent on one important check: **40 ft 9 in + 74 ft 11 in = 115 ft 8 in**. If street level is assigned local model height zero, the field would be −12.4206 m and the last seating row +22.8346 m. This is a convenient local modeling convention, not a surveyed geographic elevation or proof that the surrounding concourse is perfectly level.

The full catalog also retains locker-room areas, office dimensions, 15 press-room areas, 43 Founders/Level-5 suite areas, display dimensions and older event-space sizes. These help check scale and room proportions. **A 700 ft² room area does not tell us its wall lengths or shape.**

## Conflicts and scope traps to keep visible

1. **Historic field versus current field.** S04 gives a 684 × 345 ft field, a 32-ft-below-grade field and a 106-ft field-to-rim height. [USC's official history](https://usctrojans.com/facilities/united-airlines-field-at-los-angeles-memorial-coliseum/2) (S06) says the field was lowered **11 ft in 1993**, the track removed and **14 new rows** added. The modern guide supplies a different depth and field envelope. Do not subtract or combine these into an imagined precise historic/current datum transformation.
2. **Torch versus cauldron height.** The 1984 description gives a torch height of **107 ft above street**; the 2024 guide gives **123 ft 6 in** for the Olympic Cauldron above street. The endpoint/datum difference is unresolved. Retain the modern guide figure as a labeled reference, not a survey-verified reconciliation.
3. **Field drawing versus field envelope.** The older drawing's transverse span of 265 ft 7 in exceeds the modern guide's larger stated width of 250 ft. Their boundaries, configurations or dates differ. No common extent has yet been established.
4. **Room-table arithmetic.** The 2024 home-locker split areas total **2,538 ft²**, but the parent is listed as **1,950 ft²**. North Office 2 is listed as **823 ft²**, while its two listed component rectangles total **828 ft²**. Those entries need clarification before they constrain geometry.
5. **Event areas versus gross floor areas.** A 2,085-ft² “Suite Level” event-space label in S02 does not describe the whole suite floor. Likewise, S05's “over 1,000,000 SF” renovation scope is not the stadium footprint. S01's 784,080-ft² / 18-acre “Coliseum” area has no defined boundary; the NPS nomination's approximately 13 acres refers to its nominated property and cannot be substituted.
6. **Levels versus seating price groups.** Tower floors, bowl seating sections, ticketing price levels and previous viewers' presentation layers are different concepts. The actual seven-floor tower layouts provide a better basis than treating every menu layer as a physical floor.
7. **Operational information versus guaranteed access.** S01's elevator matrix is valuable, but does not by itself prove a continuous accessible route, door clearances or ramp slopes. In particular, it states there is no elevator access to the locker rooms or field. A 48-inch escalator dimension is not evidence of a wheelchair-accessible clear path.

## What this enables, and what remains missing

The sources support a **dimension-anchored architectural approximation**: register the official site and floor layouts, use the historic outer-bowl dimensions as a provisional check, and use the modern field/street/rim heights to establish the overall vertical scale. Model the south tower separately using its floor layouts, exterior photographs and documented overall height. DLR Group confirms that the 2019 project preserved the existing perimeter enclosure and general bowl shape, while introducing a seven-story south tower.

This is stronger input than fitting an arbitrary oval to screenshots. It still cannot establish a measured digital twin throughout the venue. The largest remaining gaps are:

- Current perimeter survey and geographic/vertical control points.
- Tower footprint dimensions, as-built sections and actual floor-to-floor elevations.
- Changes between the older field drawing and the 2024 operating configuration.
- Peristyle arch widths, depths, heights and cauldron structural geometry.
- Reliable as-built bowl profiles at multiple stations, seating risers/treads and aisle geometry.
- Complete under-stand spaces, tunnels, concourses, ramps, doors and support structures.
- Any LA28 temporary overlays or subsequent modifications absent from the reviewed documents.

For the next research step, prioritize the Huntington's drawing inventory, a higher-resolution/dimensioned site plan from the venue, and the 2019 as-built tower sections from USC or its design team. The [April 2016 Commission packet](https://assets-us-01.kc-usercontent.com/0234f496-d2b7-00b6-17a4-b43e949b70a2/e4d96d5a-0dce-4149-a7b4-2455464bb2bf/160428%20Regular%20Meeting%20Agenda%20Packet.pdf) (S07), PDF pp. 89–90, requires historic recordation including site plans, sketch floor plans and exterior/interior photography. That is a lead for another record collection; this research did not verify that the resulting documentation is publicly available or deposited with the Library of Congress.

## Research and catalog notes

- Web discovery used search results as leads; the cited numerical measurements were checked against downloaded source documents. PDF page numbers are one-based and distinguishable from their printed labels.
- Some State Exposition Park document URLs returned HTTP 403. City Clerk PDF URLs returned an HTML challenge page with HTTP 200, not the advertised PDF; they were not used as evidence. The public Commission packets provided a separate accessible primary source.
- A “View Project in 3D” item appears in the DLR page's hidden template markup. No functioning Coliseum model endpoint was recovered there; this is not evidence that a downloadable model exists.
- S03 provides areas in a pandemic operations plan. Only area columns were transcribed; occupancy limits were not mistaken for dimensions. Ambiguous merged-cell Loge entries were excluded.
- Repeated facts were generally retained once. For example, matching press-room areas in S01 and S03 do not represent separate measurements. This catalog is broad, but is not a transcription of every equipment size or temporary event layout in every PDF.
- Measurements are **source-reported**, not independently surveyed. A government archive or official venue guide can contain errors; the conflicts above remain unresolved. No dimensions were estimated from pixels in this catalog.
- Local downloaded documents and search responses are kept in ignored `raw/`, `text/` and `searches/` directories. `sources.csv` records the selected reference files' hashes and sizes. The shareable ZIP contains our catalog, findings and explorer, and links to the publishers' documents.
- Rebuild the CSV files and standalone explorer with `python research/coliseum-dimensions/build_catalog.py` from the repository root. This regenerates the catalog from the reviewed transcriptions, without re-scraping websites.
